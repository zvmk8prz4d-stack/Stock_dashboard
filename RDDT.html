PART 1 — Institutional introductory report

1.1 Business Model

Reddit operates a community platform ("subreddits") built on content generated for free by its users. The model is ad-supported with revenue almost fully dependent on advertising: in Q1 2026, ads delivered $625M of total $663M revenue (94.3% of the top line) VERIFIED. The "Other revenue" line ($39M, +15% YoY) includes data licensing to AI companies — publicly disclosed partners are Google and OpenAI VERIFIED — CNBC Q1 2026. Geographically, revenue remains US-centric, but international is growing faster (+76% YoY in Q1 vs +67% US), with direct expansion in North America, Europe and Australia and partner channels elsewhere.

1.2 Revenue Drivers

Q1 +69% YoY growth is driven by both impressions and pricing expanding in parallel. Performance advertising (measurable campaigns: purchases, installs, sign-ups) crossed 60% of ad revenue mix for the first time — historically a higher-margin mix than brand advertising. Global ARPU $5.23 (vs $4.81 estimate), US ARPU $9.63 (vs $8.53 estimate); US ARPU is nearly 2x global, confirming international monetization is still early-innings.

DAUq (Daily Active Uniques) 126.8M (+17% YoY), US DAUq 53.5M (+7%). Management explicitly set a 100M US DAUq target (roughly double) without a timeline. Starting Q3 2026, Reddit will discontinue the logged-in vs logged-out breakdown — a signal that the metric had become a narrative battleground with bears.

1.3 Cost Structure

The model is structurally capital-light: Q1 2026 capex = $1M on $663M revenue (0.2%). On the same day, Alphabet raised 2026 capex guidance to $180-190B and Meta to $125-145B. R&D TTM $789M (32% of revenue) and SG&A ~34% — still heavy in absolute terms but declining as a percentage thanks to operating leverage. Gross margin TTM 90.7%, among the highest in public tech — reflecting the zero-cost UGC model VERIFIED — TradingView 2026-05-23. SBC (stock-based comp) remains a pressure point: management guided Q2 SBC growth to roughly half the revenue growth rate — disciplined but still material.

1.4 Competitive Advantages (Moat)

Three cumulative advantages of varying depth:

  1. Network effect on UGC — more active subreddits attract more users, more users generate more content, classic virtuous loop. Depth: high, but replicable (Meta launched Forum in May 2026 targeting exactly topic-based communities) VERIFIED — Quiver 2026-05-22.
  2. Data moat for LLM training — over 25 billion posts and comments, almost all human-generated and identifiable, indexed by subreddit/topic. For LLMs it's "fuel" — Huffman's phrasing, consistent with Google/OpenAI contracts. Depth: high near-term, vulnerable long-term: if data licensing prices compress or LLMs build alternative datasets (forum archives, video-to-text), the moat weakens.
  3. SEO authority — Reddit appears in top-3 Google results for thousands of informal queries. Under direct attack: AI Overviews and AI Mode answer in-SERP before any click. This is the structural risk (see 1.5).

1.5 Key Risks

The dominant risk is singular, name it openly: Google AI Overviews and AI Mode can erode logged-out traffic, which today accounts for 40-50% of Reddit's total traffic per Wells Fargo estimates VERIFIED — Wells Fargo reports May 2025/2026. Redburn Atlantic's bear case ($75 target) rests entirely on this. Reddit's response is building "Reddit Answers" (its own internal AI-search) and shifting weight toward logged-in users (more monetizable).

Other material risks:

  • Customer concentration: top-15 advertising verticals, exposed to a macro ad cycle. A US recession → ad budgets cut first.
  • Class action lawsuit (filed N.D. Cal., class period Oct 2024 - May 2025) for misleading disclosure on Google AI impact VERIFIED — SimplyWall.st May 2026. Not material for the P&L, but a narrative drag.
  • Key-person CEO: Steve Huffman co-founder + CEO. Long tenure but high dependence on the sole public face.
  • Dual-class share structure: Class A (NYSE listed) vs Class B (insider, disproportionate voting power). Limits hostile takeover but also board discipline.
  • EU/US antitrust: no direct exposure now, but if Reddit becomes indispensable for LLM training, regulatory scrutiny rises.

1.6 Management Quality

Steve Huffman (CEO, co-founder 2005, second tenure since 2015): earnings call tone is specific, numerical, not vague. "When you look across the more than 300 publicly traded tech companies, there's only one that combines this type of growth, profitability and efficiency, and that's Reddit" — Q1 2026 call. Such an explicit assertion of superiority is rare in tech C-suite; it needs to be tested against numbers (see Section 5.1, and largely it holds).

Capital allocation: $1B buyback authorized Feb 5, 2026, the first-ever for RDDT VERIFIED — SEC 8-K. Capex minimal. No dividend (appropriate for growth stage). Cash $2.77B + ST investments against $304M total liabilities = extremely strong financial position.

Insider ownership and Form 4 (see 6.4): pattern dominated by routine 10b5-1 sales from Huffman, Wong (COO), Slowe (CTO), Reynolds (CAO), Vollero (CFO) — 339+ sales vs 4 buys over the last 6 months. Important exception: director Sarah Farrell made 4 open-market purchases totaling 61,000 shares at ~$145 average ($8.87M cumulative) between February and May 2026 — a convergent buyer that merits attention (see 6.4).

1.7 Sector Position

Reddit is a mid-cap pure-play in social/communities, in a sector dominated by Meta ($1.7T), Google ($2T+), with Pinterest ($25B) and Snap ($15B) as closest comparables. Global digital ad market share is still <1%, but within a sub-segment (community-based authentic content) Reddit is the US absolute leader. New competition comes from:

  • Meta Forum (launched May 2026) — model imitation, Meta distribution scale but no authenticity moat;
  • TikTok for attention/time, not for text content;
  • Substack/Discord for niche communities, but with more limited horizontal reach.

1.8 Short and long-term outlook

Next 4 quarters (Q2-Q4 2026, Q1 2027): consensus FY26 revenue +46.5% YoY, FY26 EPS +89% YoY. Comp difficulty rises from Q3 (anniversary of the first quarter of true operating leverage), so reported growth will arithmetically decelerate — this is the dominant narrative risk for H2 2026.

3-5 years: the thesis is binary. If management achieves 100M US DAUq (current double) while maintaining ARPU growth, the company can deliver $5-7B revenue at 35-40% FCF margins — fair value $300-400. If Google AI hollows 30-40% of logged-out traffic without Reddit replacing it via owned product, top-line decelerates to ~20% and the multiple compresses to 15x EV/EBITDA — fair value $90-110. The bracket is enormous: this is a bimodal-return equity, not Gaussian.

PART 3 — Bull vs Bear

BULL — The long thesis

  1. Exceptional growth at a reasonable multiple post-correction. Forward EPS growth +89% YoY with forward P/E 28.7x and PEG 0.10. EV/EBITDA TTM 38.5x is high in absolute terms but drops to ~22-24x on FY26 forward EBITDA (estimated FCF ~$1.2-1.4B). For a pure-play with +70% revenue growth and 35% FCF margin, this is competitive vs Meta (+22% growth, ~10x EV/EBITDA) if Reddit sustains the pace for another 6+ quarters.
  2. Fortress balance sheet: $2.77B cash, $21M total debt. Net debt/EBITDA −4.3x (net cash). $1B buyback authorized — covers ~3.7% of market cap at current prices, real multiple-compression optionality.
  3. Convergent insider buying: director Sarah Farrell purchased 61,000 shares for $8.87M between Feb and May 2026 at ~$145 average. For a director, this is a rare own-money commitment. See 6.4.
  4. Short squeeze potential: short interest 14.7% of float, days-to-cover 3.4. If Q2 beats and Google traffic does not deteriorate in reported metrics, forced covering can deliver a quick +20-30%.
  5. Analyst optimism: 32 analysts, average target $217-225, range $75-300. Selection bias (sell-side loses commissions on heavy downgrades), but 17 analysts hold targets ≥ $215 — not isolated.
  6. Data licensing optionality: $39M Q1 (annualized $156M) from Google + OpenAI. If 2026/2027 renewals price LLM-grade data scarcity, this line can double with no marginal cost.

BEAR — The short / avoid thesis

  1. Google AI Search is an unquantified structural risk. 40-50% of traffic is logged-out, predominantly Google-sourced. AI Overviews and AI Mode answer in-SERP without click-through. Wells Fargo cut 2026 ad revenue 6%, 2027 14%. Redburn Atlantic holds a $75 target (below the $99 52w low). This is not speculation, it's a thesis held by primary firms with long-standing coverage.
  2. Arithmetic comp difficulty from Q3 2026. Q3-Q4 2025 were quarters of strong operating leverage (EBITDA margin from 21% to 45% in 12 months). Replicating those deltas is arithmetically impossible. Reported growth must decelerate. The market rewards decelerating-growth names from 70% to 50% only when the multiple was already compressed — here it isn't.
  3. Disclosure step-back: dropping logged-in vs logged-out from Q3 2026 removes the most diagnostic Google-risk metric from analysts. A confident management would not have chosen this timing.
  4. Massive insider selling: 343 sales vs 4 buys over 6 months. Huffman 216,000 shares for $39M, Wong 339,470 for $61M, Slowe 173,000 for $32M. While 10b5-1, the volume at $140-180 prices signals no urgency to accumulate.
  5. High historical multiple vs nearest peer: see Section 5.1. Pinterest, Snap trade at 3-5x P/S; Meta at 8x; Reddit at 12.3x P/S — 50%+ premium vs Meta. Justified only if growth remains superior.
  6. Meta Forum is a distribution threat: Meta has 3B+ DAU vs Reddit's 126M. Forum is inferior on authenticity, but the acquisition funnel is massive.
  7. Class action lawsuit: narrative drag, legal costs, potential settlement.

3.1 Declared winner

The bull thesis does not refute the bear thesis; the bear wins as a near-term risk priority. The question is not "is Reddit a quality company?" (it is) but "does the $141 price discount enough of the Google risk?". Three considerations:

  • Drawdown already 50% from ATH. The market has started pricing the risk, but hasn't yet priced a Wells Fargo bear case ($120) or a Redburn bear case ($75).
  • Q1 was a wide beat: if Q2 (Aug 11, 2026) confirms logged-in DAUq growth >15% YoY, the logged-out bear case weakens. If Q2 shows logged-in DAUq deceleration, the price moves to $100.
  • The technical setup (Part 7) suggests further weakness near-term.

Conclusion: the bear carries more weight today. A long position now requires conviction that Q2 will deliver a second material beat; without that conviction, waiting is the better choice. The most rational entry level is a correction toward $110-125 (see 7.6).
