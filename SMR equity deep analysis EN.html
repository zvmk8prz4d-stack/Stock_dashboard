1.Institutional Introductory Report

1.1 — Business Model

NuScale Power designs and sells the NuScale Power Module (NPM), a 77 MWe light-water small modular reactor that can be aggregated up to 12 units into VOYGR plants (~924 MWe). It is the only SMR design certified by the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission. VERIFIED — 10-Q Q1 2026, IR site

The revenue model is NOT yet that of a power producer: today NuScale sells technology licensing, engineering services and long-lead materials to project partners. Revenue is therefore lumpy and milestone-driven, not recurring. VERIFIED — 10-Q

Founded in 2007 in Corvallis, Oregon. Founding sponsor Fluor is exiting the equity (see 6.4). Exclusive global strategic partner: ENTRA1 Energy. VERIFIED

1.2 — Revenue Drivers

Three potential drivers, all still prospective: (1) the ENTRA1/TVA 6 GW program — the largest US SMR program, with a Power Purchase Agreement expected by end-2026; (2) Romania's RoPower 6-module project, with a go/no-go decision (FID) at mid-2026; (3) international orders (Japan, South Korea) facilitated by government frameworks. TTM revenue ~$31.5M; Q1 2026 collapsed to $0.565M (−95.5% YoY) due to the wind-down of cash-generating projects in 2025. VERIFIED — Q1 2026 earnings call

1.3 — Cost Structure

Typical pre-commercialization cost base: R&D, engineering, supply chain (a 37-supplier summit, modules produced by Doosan Enerbility, Framatome partnership). Q1 2026 operating loss −$57.5M; quarterly operating cash burn −$314.7M (inflated by the $264.2M paydown of accounts payable on long-lead materials, hence not representative of the run-rate). Normalized opex run-rate ~$200–230M/year. VERIFIED — 8-K Q1 2026

1.4 — Competitive Advantages (Moat)

  • Regulatory advantage: the only SMR with an NRC-certified design — a real entry barrier, hard to replicate near term. VERIFIED
  • Nuclear supply-chain first-mover: established relationship with Doosan and Framatome for modules. VERIFIED — Q1 call
  • Strong balance sheet: ~$1.0–1.15B liquidity, zero long-term debt — multi-year runway that most pre-revenue peers lack. VERIFIED — 10-Q
  • Moat limit: the regulatory edge has not yet translated into firm orders. Peers (Oklo, Nano Nuclear) are signing deals while NuScale remains on exploratory arrangements. INFERRED — market discussion

1.5 — Key Risks

  • Binary commercialization risk: the first operational SMR reactor is unlikely before 2030; profits follow years later. VERIFIED — sector notes
  • Dilution: funded via ATM equity issuance ($500M shelf). Class A shares went from 122.8M (end-2024) to 323.7M (Q1 2026). VERIFIED — 10-Q, 8-K
  • Sponsor exit: Fluor sold ~111.4M shares (~$1.83B) over the past 6 months. VERIFIED — Form 4 / QuiverQuant
  • Partner dependence: the thesis hinges on ENTRA1 and TVA for financing and execution of the 6 GW; PPA slippage keeps a lid on the stock. VERIFIED — Motley Fool, Q1 call
  • Policy risk: the narrative is fueled by Trump's nuclear agenda; a political or federal-budget slowdown would reverse sentiment. INFERRED

1.6 — Management Quality

CEO John Hopkins, CFO Robert Ramsey Hamady (talks of a "fortress balance sheet"), COO Carl Fisher, CTO and co-founder Jose Reyes, CCO Clayton Scott. Material governance signal: over the past 6 months every insider only sold (0 purchases). In a pre-revenue company asking the market for patience, the absence of open-market buying by management is a weak alignment signal. VERIFIED — Form 4 / QuiverQuant

1.7 — Sector Position

NuScale is the "blue-chip" SMR name thanks to NRC certification, but it competes in a crowded field: Oklo (OKLO) targets faster commercialization with a fast-reactor design and signed a deal with Meta; Nano Nuclear (NNE); and the traditional nuclear majors (Constellation, Vistra, GE Vernova, BWX Technologies, Cameco). The competitive risk is execution and timing, not technology. VERIFIED — TipRanks, Motley Fool

1.8 — Outlook

Short term (2-3 quarters): dominated by two events: the RoPower FID at mid-2026 and the ENTRA1/TVA PPA expected end-2026. Without them, the numbers stay pre-revenue with losses and burn. VERIFIED

3-5 years: if the PPA materializes and RoPower advances, NuScale could become the only SMR with firm orders and a ready supply chain — an option on a huge market (AI data centers, decarbonization). If it slips, it is a cash burner that dilutes. UNCERTAIN — depends on catalysts that have not yet occurred

3 bullish reasons

  1. Only NRC-certified SMR design + balance sheet with ~$1B liquidity and zero long-term debt.
  2. ENTRA1/TVA PPA catalyst by end-2026: if signed, it unlocks the timeline of the 6 GW program.
  3. Policy tailwind (Trump executive orders, 400 GW by 2050 target) and AI data-center power demand.

3 bearish reasons

  1. Revenue collapsed to $0.565M in Q1 2026; real commercialization likely not before 2030.
  2. Ongoing ATM dilution + Fluor sponsor exit (~$1.83B sold) + insiders 100% selling.
  3. Broken technical trend: price −45.9% below SMA200, −78.8% from the high.

Balanced conclusion

SMR is a "story-versus-stats" stock. The stats today are ugly: pre-revenue, loss-making, diluting, with management selling. The story is powerful: NRC regulatory monopoly, solid balance sheet, an incoming PPA catalyst and a megatrend (nuclear + AI) behind it. The honest equilibrium is that this is not a "fundamental confirmation" position for a disciplined medium-long-term investor, but a binary thematic bet to be sized like an option, not a core holding. The price already discounts plenty of pessimism, but dilution and catalyst-slippage risk keep the risk/reward only marginally positive.
